Remote Operations Support

Connect to your Mac

On your Mac, open System Settings, General, Sharing. Turn on Remote Login for the Terminal and Screen Sharing for the desktop view. Then add your Mac in the app with its address on your network (Tailscale, your VPN, or the same Wi-Fi).

It cannot connect

Check that your iPhone or iPad and your Mac are on the same network or both signed in to Tailscale, that the Mac is awake, and that Remote Login and Screen Sharing are on.
