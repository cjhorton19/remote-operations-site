Remote Operations

Use your Mac from your iPhone and iPad, like a touch screen. Private by design: your connection goes straight to your Mac and never touches our servers.

What you can do

  • See and control your Mac's desktop with natural touch: tap to click, drag to scroll.
  • Open a Terminal on your Mac over SSH.
  • Keep a list of your Macs and connect with one tap.

How it connects

The app connects directly to a Mac you own, over your own network: Tailscale (recommended), another VPN you run, or the same Wi-Fi. There is no account and no relay server in between.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
